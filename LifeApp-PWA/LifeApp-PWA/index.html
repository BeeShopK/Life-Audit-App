Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device. LifeApp does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

Everything you enter — quiz answers, journal entries, mood check-ins, and account details — is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage.

When you use the AI Coach feature, your typed message is sent to the Anthropic Claude API over a secure connection. No other personal data (name, quiz scores, journal) is sent to Anthropic.

You can clear all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Clear All Data.
